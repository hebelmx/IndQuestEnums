ADR-0003 • Opted Out net10.0;net11.0 Enabled

IndQuestEnums — C# 15 / .NET 11 Union & Closed Hierarchies Due Diligence

Date: 2026-11-15 • Host: Abel Briones • BMAD Party Mode v6.12.4

1. Cross-Assembly Block

public closed abstract class restricts inheritance to the declaring assembly, preventing Domain projects from deriving from EnumModel.

2. Value vs. Type Identity

EnumModel instances are static readonly singletons of one sealed class, whereas closed/union require a distinct CLR Type per member.

3. Converter & Factory Break

Subclass-per-member breaks where TEnum : new(), GetAll<TEnum>() field reflection, EF EnumModelConverter, and EnumModelJsonConverter.

4. Source Gen Pragmatism

Evaluated pre-agentic era; keeping EnumModel dependency-free and multi-targeted (net10.0;net11.0) delivers maximum ROI with zero ceremony.
